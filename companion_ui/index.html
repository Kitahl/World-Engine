Connect ChatGPT to this PC

What is ngrok? It gives World Engine a stable, secure HTTPS address so your private GPT Action can reach the engine running locally. It is not required for this desktop companion.

  1. Open the official ngrok account page and sign in.
  2. Copy your one-time authtoken. If the site copy button fails, select the token and press Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste it below. World Engine validates and stores it without logging or returning it.

The final one-time GPT Builder schema and Bearer-key import cannot be changed by a local program. World Engine will prepare the files and tell you exactly what remains.
